# Video-AI Ad Prompt — "Enable Microsoft Intune, Free" (Workplace lead-gen)

Source product: **Enable Microsoft Intune — Quick-Start (New or Existing Tenant)** — the free lead-gen
preview on **Phoenixtekk Workplace** (workplace.phoenixtekk.com). Use these prompts in Veo, Sora, Kling,
Runway, or Pika. A **master prompt** (single block) is provided first, then a **scene-by-scene
storyboard**, **voiceover script**, and **style/audio/technical direction**. Two ratios: **16:9** (web/
YouTube pre-roll) and **9:16** (Reels/TikTok/Shorts).

---

## 1. MASTER PROMPT (paste-as-one-block)

> A sleek 30-second tech product ad, cinematic and modern, deep navy-blue background with electric-green
> and sky-blue accents, clean glassmorphic UI cards floating in 3D space, subtle circuit-line motifs and
> soft particle glow. Cool, confident, "enterprise but effortless" mood. The story: an overwhelmed IT
> administrator turns a chaotic pile of unmanaged laptops and phones into a calm, organized, secure fleet
> by enabling Microsoft Intune — for free. Open on a stressed admin at a messy desk with red warning icons
> ("No device management," "Unmanaged," "No enrollment") hovering over scattered devices. A bright
> green arrow sweeps across the frame and the scene transforms: devices snap into a neat grid, each
> lighting up with a green check; a Windows laptop and an iPhone rise to the center, both stamped
> "Enrolled · Compliant." Crisp kinetic typography reveals "Enable Microsoft Intune — FREE." End on a
> premium end card: a glowing shield check, the word "Workplace," the line "SIMPLE. FAST. FREE.," and the
> URL workplace.phoenixtekk.com with a "Get it free" button. Smooth camera pushes and parallax, tasteful
> depth of field, high-end motion-graphics polish, no clutter, no text errors, legible sans-serif type.
> Upbeat minimal electronic underscore with a satisfying "power-on" whoosh on the transformation and a
> soft chime on the final check. 30 seconds.

**Negative / avoid:** warped or gibberish text, extra fingers, fake Microsoft/Apple logos, watermarks,
cluttered UI, more than a few words of on-screen text per beat, cheesy stock-corporate handshake imagery.

> New tenant or existing — turning on device management usually means a consultant, or a week of trial
> and error. Not anymore. A free, click-by-click guide gets Microsoft Intune running the right way. Enroll
> a Windows PC and an iPhone, end to end — and know it works. It's free, and a preview of everything the
> Workplace catalog can do next. Enable Intune, free. Only on Workplace.

**VO direction:** confident, warm, unhurried; mid-tempo; a knowing "we've got you" tone — not hypey.
Neutral North-American accent. Slight lift on "free" and on "Workplace."

## 4. STYLE, AUDIO & TECHNICAL DIRECTION

**Visual identity (match the product hero):**
- Palette: deep navy `#1F3A5F` background · electric green `#2ECC71`-family for success/accents · sky
  blue `#2E86C1` for secondary. White type.
- Look: glassmorphic cards, thin circuit lines, soft particle glow, restrained. "Enterprise but
  effortless." High contrast, legible, uncluttered.
- Motion: smooth camera pushes/parallax, snappy card reveals, one hero "transformation" moment (Scene 2).
- Icons/metaphors: red ⚠️ → green ✓; shield-with-check = secure/compliant; a Windows laptop + an iPhone
  as the two hero devices.

**Audio:**
- Music: minimal upbeat electronic underscore, building subtly to the end card.
- SFX: a "power-on" whoosh at the Scene-2 transformation; soft ticks as each step-card checks; a clean
  chime on the final shield-check.

**Technical:**
- Duration 30s (also cut a 15s: keep Scenes 1→2→4→6).
- Ratios: **16:9** master; export a **9:16** vertical (see below) and a **1:1** for feed.
- Frame rate 24–30fps; clean, sharp, no film grain overload.
- Keep all on-screen text to ≤4 words per beat to avoid AI text warping; overlay the URL/CTA as a
  post-production graphic if the model garbles small text.

**Compliance / brand safety:**
- Do **not** render real Microsoft or Apple logos or trademarked UI — imply with generic laptop/phone
  silhouettes and the words "Windows" / "iPhone." "Microsoft Intune" as spoken/typed product name only.
- This is a **branded** ad for Phoenixtekk Workplace (the white-label rule applies to the *product docs*,
  not to marketing of the marketplace). Use the "Workplace" wordmark + workplace.phoenixtekk.com.
