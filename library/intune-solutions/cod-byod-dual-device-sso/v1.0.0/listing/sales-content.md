# Sales Content — Dual-Device (Personal + Corporate) Management with Single Sign-On

## Hook
**One account, single sign-on, across a user's personal and corporate device.**

Built for the employee who carries two devices — a personal phone (BYOD) and a corporate laptop (COD).
The same Microsoft Entra account signs in on **both**, with seamless single sign-on; IT fully manages
the corporate device and protects corporate data on the personal one — at the same time, for the same
person, in Microsoft Intune.
