# Sales Content — Corporate-Owned (COD) & BYOD Device Management

## Hook
**One identity across every device — company-owned and personal.**

Manage corporate-owned (COD) and personal (BYOD) devices in Microsoft Intune under a single Microsoft
Entra identity. The same account signs in on both; IT applies full control where it owns the device
and data-only protection where the employee does.
