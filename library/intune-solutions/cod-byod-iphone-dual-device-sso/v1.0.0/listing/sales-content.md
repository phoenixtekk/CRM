# Sales Content — Dual-Device iPhone (Personal + Corporate iOS) Management with Single Sign-On

## Hook
**One account, single sign-on, across a user's personal and corporate iPhone.**

Built for the employee who carries two iPhones — a personal iPhone (BYOD) and a company iPhone (COD).
The same Microsoft Entra account signs in on **both**, with seamless single sign-on; IT fully manages
the corporate iPhone (supervised via Apple Business Manager + ADE) and protects corporate data on the
personal one — at the same time, for the same person, in Microsoft Intune.
