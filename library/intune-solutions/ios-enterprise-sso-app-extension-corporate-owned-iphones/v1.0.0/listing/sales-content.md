# Sales Content — iOS/iPadOS Enterprise SSO App Extension for Corporate-Owned iPhones

## Hook
**Stop the sign-in prompts and repeat MFA on corporate iPhones.** Deploy the Microsoft Enterprise SSO
plug-in as an Apple Extensible SSO app extension via Intune — silent, shared sign-in to Entra-secured
apps and Safari across every corporate-owned iPhone and iPad.

## The problem
Corporate iPhones are enrolled and managed, but users still re-authenticate over and over — a password
prompt here, an MFA challenge there, once per app and again in Safari. The fix isn't another policy or
a helpdesk script; it's the **Enterprise SSO app extension** that most tenants simply never deploy on
iOS. On iOS the extension lives inside **Microsoft Authenticator** (not Company Portal, as on the Mac),
and a single coverage key can extend SSO to **every managed app** at once — details teams routinely
miss.
