Microsoft 365 License Optimization & Reharvesting

Microsoft 365 licenses are assigned per user (directly or via group-based licensing) and billed per seat every month whether or not the seat is used. Over time the assigned count drifts above the needed count: people leave but keep their seats, users are placed on a high tier (E5) when a lower tier (E3) covers what they actually do, and duplicate or overlapping plans accumulate.
