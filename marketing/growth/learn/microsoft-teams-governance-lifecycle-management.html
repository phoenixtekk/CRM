Microsoft Teams Governance & Lifecycle Management

Microsoft Teams Governance & Lifecycle Management is a run-to-implement solution that puts an enterprise back in control of how Microsoft Teams is created, named, classified, owned, and retired. Every team is backed by a Microsoft 365 Group, so "Teams governance" is really governance of the underlying M365 Groups plus the Teams-specific surfaces (channels, archival, reporting). Without governance, Teams grows into sprawl: anyone can spin up a team, duplicates multiply, owners leave and are never replaced, and data ages indefinitely with no expiration or retention decision behind it.
