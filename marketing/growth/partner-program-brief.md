# MSP / Partner Program — brief

**Why this is your highest-leverage channel:** the entire catalog is already **white-label** (branding
off by default). An MSP can buy a runbook once and deliver it to *their* clients under *their* brand — you
sell one asset, they resell it many times. Lowest customer-acquisition cost, highest lifetime value, and
it scales your reach through other people's client bases.

## The offer (starting point — tune to your economics)
- **What partners get:** the full click-by-click runbook + tested scripts for any solution, **white-label**
  (no Phoenixtekk branding — it already ships that way), to use across all their managed clients.
- **Reseller / volume options** (pick a model):
  1. **Per-solution partner price** — a standing discount off list for verified partners.
  2. **Catalog subscription** — a monthly/annual seat that unlocks the whole library for the partner's team
     (you already have the subscription tiers scaffolded in `catalog/`).
  3. **Deal-reg / co-delivery** — partner brings the client, you co-deliver the harder engagements.
- **Unlimited devices** per solution (already the model) makes this trivially attractive to an MSP with many clients.

## Who to target
- **Microsoft-focused MSPs / MSSPs** (Intune/Entra/Defender/Purview practices).
- **Boutique modern-workplace & identity consultancies** that need runbooks their junior engineers can execute.
- **Public-sector integrators** — your **GCC High** variants are a genuine differentiator most competitors lack.

## The pitch (one-liner)
> "Deliver senior-level Microsoft runbooks to every client — under your brand — without hiring senior
> engineers. 400+ white-label, click-to-implement solutions. Buy once, deploy across all your clients."

## Assets in this channel
- **`/partners` page** (deployed): the pitch + a partner-intake form (tagged `src=partner`, lands in your
  CRM like every other lead) so MSPs can raise their hand.
- This brief (sales-team reference).
- Next: a partner one-pager PDF, a sample white-label deliverable to show the quality, and a simple partner
  agreement.

## Proof points to lead with
- **400+ solutions · $1.1M+ catalog value** (your live homepage stat).
- **White-label by default** — verified brand-free; the partner's clients never see your name.
- **GCC High variants** — public-sector ready.
- **"Built in 1 day"** — if a partner needs a runbook you don't have yet, you build it.

## Measurement
- `/partners` form submissions arrive tagged `src=partner` → CRM (Dolibarr) + `leadgen-digest.py`.
- Track partner-sourced revenue separately (it should have the best margin and retention).
